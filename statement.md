## Project Title

Digital Evidence Chain-of-Custody Manager

## Problem Statement

Digital investigations involve collecting, storing, transferring,
and analyzing digital evidence. During this process, it is important
to maintain an accurate record of who handled evidence and when it
was transferred.

Manual tracking of evidence can result in incomplete records,
difficulty in verification, and uncertainty regarding whether an
evidence file has been modified.

The Digital Evidence Chain-of-Custody Manager provides a Java-based
system for managing investigation cases and digital evidence while
maintaining chain-of-custody records and verifying evidence integrity
using SHA-256 hashing.

## Scope

The project covers:

- Investigation case management
- Digital evidence registration
- SHA-256 integrity verification
- Evidence transfer tracking
- Chain-of-custody management
- User authentication
- Role-based authorization
- Evidence searching
- Audit logging
- Persistent local storage
